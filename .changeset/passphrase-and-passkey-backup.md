A BIP-39 passphrase, backups under a passkey, and the device credential before Android 11. `vault.unlock(id, {prompt, passphrase})` opens the wallet the words make under a passphrase, which crosses as bytes the vault overwrites, is NFKD-normalized natively and is never stored. `createVault(crypto, {relyingParty})` names the app's associated domain, and `vault.backup(id, {passkey: {name} | true, prompt})` seals an entry under a passkey's PRF output (iOS 18, Android 9 through Credential Manager), which goes from the platform to the engine without reaching JavaScript; `vault.restore(id, backup, {passkey: true})` opens it with the passkey the person picks. A backup's kind is read before anything is asked (`vault.backupKind`), and the wrong secret is refused with `NEEDS_PASSKEY` or `NEEDS_PASSWORD`. New error codes `WRONG_PASSKEY`, `PASSKEY_UNSUPPORTED` and `PASSKEY_NOT_FOUND`; `vault.passkeySupported` says whether the system and engine have what it takes (ADR 0003). On Android 7 to 10, an entry opened with the passcode too gets a key that works for 5 seconds after the person authenticates with biometrics or the device credential, instead of biometrics only (ADR 0004). Needs `@hzblj/nitro-crypto` 0.1.5.
